## QR menu / order & pay

Claims 5000+ restaurants; customer quotes: 10% top-line, 22% tip rate, 7-12 min saved per table, 5x 5-star Google reviews, 60% guest emails captured. Pricing: sales-led, not public.
- **me&u (ex Mr Yum)** https://www.meandu.com — Order & Pay, Pay, multi-vendor food hall (one payment), hybrid guest/server, Staff Upsells app (Crew), "For You" AI recommendations, trending items, taste sorting (dynamic item positions), Reservations (Quick Dine, AI assistant, shift briefs, ad attribution), Voice AI phone agent (handles 70-80% of calls, local voices), Functions/events, CRM (Connect), Loyalty, Gift cards, Velocity frequent-flyer points, APIs (POS, loyalty, order data, data ingestion), 70+ POS integrations, 24/7 human support. Claims 28m+ users, 6,000+ locations. Pricing not public.
- **qlub** https://qlub.io — Pay-at-table QR, order-and-pay, digital menu, payment links for catering/events, tips prompts, loyalty, Google review boosts, bill splitting, reconciliation. 3,000+ venues (UAE-based, +971). Sales-led pricing.
- **MENU TIGER** https://www.menutiger.com/pricing — Free US$0 (75 orders/mo), Regular $17 (500), Advanced $46 (2,000, KDS, promos, order tracking), Premium $119 (unlimited, POS, floor plan, split bills, white label). - **GoTab** https://gotab.com/pricing — Basic $15/mo, Pro $99, Sync $229 (offline on-prem server) per location. QR order & pay with shared tab across staff + guest, kiosk, KDS, handheld POS, loyalty+memberships, gift cards, dynamic pricing rules & coupons, 3rd-party aggregation, Opsi back office (invoice processing, recipes, inventory, tasks), Fishbowl guest marketing, "Lovey" AI analytics assistant. Card processing from 2.40%+$0.15. 0% hardware financing 24-36 months, Hardware Flex Leasing. 24/7 support, onboarding incl. menu setup & training.
- **Flipdish** https://www.flipdish.com — branded website & app, loyalty, automated upsell, delivery network (Uber Direct, Stuart), AI marketing (PPC, SMS, social), POS, kiosk, KDS, marketplace menu sync, AI demand forecasting & staff scheduling, AI phone agents, menu optimisation ML, dedicated onboarding + customer success. 5000+ brands.
